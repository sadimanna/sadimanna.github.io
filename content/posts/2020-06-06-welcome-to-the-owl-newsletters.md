Hey friends,

We are excited to announce that we are adding Newsletter to our publication as a means to reach our readers more easily and expand our audience. Furthermore, our subscribers will receive emails about new stories on our Publication or updates, and they will also be able to communicate with us via emails.

The Owl is considerably new, and has very few posts now. As you readers already know, the posts in The Owl are based on topics which are more implementation-based rather than theoretical or mathematical concepts, we will try to publish more posts which discusses new critical and intriguing topics in Machine Learning (and certainly Deep Learning!) and Artificial Intelligence, from the very basic level.

We are open to suggestions from our readers, be it about any topic you want to be published, or how we can improve the content on our Publication, or make ourselves more reachable to you.

Only by sharing what we learn, we can grow the expanse of our knowledge.

Keep Learning, Keep Sharing, Keep Growing!

The Owl
