Generative Models — Diffusion Chapter — Part 3— Putting it all together!

Diffusion Models demystified!!



Maximization of Likelihood using Score

Using only score, samples converge to modes of distribution

Add noise to encourage exploration and prevent convergence to modes

Results in Langevin equation

Connection to Fokker-Planck Equation which is a Markov process

Intractability without a path in the space of probability distribution functions

The forward process sets the path

The reverse process learns to estimate the score

This is Diffusion
